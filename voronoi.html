The Geometry of Space

You are interacting with a Voronoi Diagram and its mathematical twin, the Delaunay Triangulation.
A Voronoi diagram divides space into distinct territories, where every location inside a cell is closest to its center dot. If you connect the dots of neighboring cells, you create a Delaunay Triangulation. Physically, this creates the most "well-behaved" network of triangles possible, minimizes skinny slivers.

How does this connect to my work?
This exact geometry is the backbone of 3D computational modeling. When translating CAD models into tangible 3D environments, algorithms like Constrained Delaunay Triangulation are critical. They allow us to tessellate abstract mathematical surfaces into robust, highly accurate triangular meshes that computers can efficiently render and simulate.
